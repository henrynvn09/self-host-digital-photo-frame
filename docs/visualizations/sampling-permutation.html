1. Infinite Virtual Tape

Rounds concatenate seamlessly: Round 0 (shuffled with Seed 0) + Round 1 (shuffled with Seed 1) + Round 2 (shuffled with Seed 2). The window steps by exactly $K$ items daily.

2. Seamless Straddle Stitch

When a round ends mid-day, the frame takes the remaining items of Round $R$ (exhausting it cleanly) and fills the rest from Round $R+1$'s fresh shuffle. Zero missing photos.

3. Fresh Order Every Round

No repetition within any round. When entering Round $R+1$, photos are in an entirely new random sequence. Same-day duplicates during a straddle are automatically prevented.
